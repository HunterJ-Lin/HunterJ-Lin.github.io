Biography   

I am currently a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Xiamen University, advised by Prof. Liujuan Cao and Prof. Rongrong Ji. My research focuses on multimodal large language models, weakly and semi-supervised learning, and vision-language alignment. Recently, I focus on AI4AI.

  • 09/2023 -- Now: Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology, Xiamen University, Xiamen, China (expected graduation: 06/2027)
  • 07/2026 -- Now: Research Intern, AI4AI (Naive-N0.5-Flash), Startup, Naive AI.
  • 05/2025 -- 04/2026: Research Intern, Medical Multimodal Large Language Models (MedXIAOHE), Douyin - Xiaohe Health, Soaring Star Talent Program, ByteDance, Shanghai, China
  • 01/2024 -- 05/2024: Research Intern, Industrial Defect Detection, Intelligent Manufacturing Department, CATL, Ningde, China
  • 09/2020 -- 06/2023: M.S. in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems, Xiamen University, Xiamen, China
  • 12/2022 -- 05/2023: Research Intern, Efficient Data Annotation, Intelligent Manufacturing Department, CATL, Ningde, China
  • 02/2022 -- 08/2022: Research Intern in Open-Vocabulary Perception under the supervision of Dr. Yunhang Shen, Tencent Youtu Lab (CSIG), Shanghai, China
  • 07/2020 -- 09/2020: Development Intern, Routing Algorithm Development, Ruijie Networks, Fuzhou, China
  • 09/2016 -- 06/2020: B.S. in Software Engineering, Fuzhou University, Fuzhou, China
